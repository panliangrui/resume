I am a Ph.D. candidate in computer science at Hunan University, supervised by Prof. Shaoliang Peng. I received my master's degree from Prince of Songkla University under the supervision of Prof. Mitchai Chongcheawchamnan. My research focuses on artificial intelligence for healthcare, with particular interests in medical image analysis, computational pathology, multimodal learning, cancer bioinformatics, and precision oncology.

My recent work investigates weakly supervised learning for whole-slide images, tumor-microenvironment modeling, radiopathological fusion, graph neural networks, multi-omics representation learning, and clinically interpretable AI. I have authored or co-authored more than 20 peer-reviewed papers in journals and conferences including Nature Communications, Cancer Research, npj Precision Oncology, Information Fusion, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, IJCAI, and IEEE BIBM.
